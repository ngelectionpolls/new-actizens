Your first conversation with ASIF
ASIF website starter guide · 2026-10-18
Prepared for this website. Not an official ASIF policy, training manual or application form.

A practical checklist to help you introduce your skills, interests and availability.

INTRODUCE YOURSELF
- Your name and preferred contact email.
- The town or state where you are based.
- The community or issue you would like to support.

WHAT YOU CAN CONTRIBUTE
- List skills you are comfortable using: organising, communication, research, training, administration or digital work.
- Describe any relevant experience without including confidential information.
- Explain when you are available and whether you prefer remote or in-person participation.

QUESTIONS TO ASK
- Which volunteering opportunities are currently available?
- What eligibility checks, training or preparation are required?
- Who will be my point of contact?
- Are there costs, travel arrangements or accessibility requirements to discuss?

BEFORE YOU COMMIT
- Confirm the role, expectations, dates and practical arrangements directly with ASIF.
- This checklist helps you prepare an enquiry; it is not an application form or confirmation of a place.

Contact ASIF: info@actizensfoundation.org

Community conversation planner
ASIF website starter guide · 2026-10-18
Prepared for this website. Not an official ASIF policy, training manual or application form.

Turn a broad community worry into a conversation that gets somewhere.

DEFINE THE CONVERSATION
- What is the issue, in one sentence?
- Who is affected, and whose perspective has not yet been heard?
- What should people understand or decide by the end?

PLAN PARTICIPATION
- Choose an accessible time and location.
- Invite people with different perspectives and explain the purpose in advance.
- Agree on respectful discussion and ask before recording or sharing anyone’s contribution.

KEEP A RECORD
- Note the questions raised, the areas of agreement and the points that remain uncertain.
- Separate direct observations from assumptions.
- Avoid including private details that are not needed for the agreed next step.

AGREE ON WHAT HAPPENS NEXT
- Write down a small, specific action, a willing point of contact and a date to check progress.
- If you want support from ASIF, share the topic, location and type of help you are seeking.

Event attendance checklist
ASIF website starter guide · 2026-10-18
Prepared for this website. Not an official ASIF policy, training manual or application form.

Keep registration, travel and accessibility questions in one place before an activity.

CONFIRM THE ESSENTIALS
- Event name:
- Confirmed date and start time:
- Venue, meeting point or online joining link:
- Organiser contact:

BEFORE MAKING ARRANGEMENTS
- Confirm that registration is open and ask how your place will be acknowledged.
- Ask whether a fee applies and what is included.
- Check travel, accessibility and any equipment or materials you should bring.
- Do not treat a website listing or calendar entry as confirmation of your booking.

DURING THE ACTIVITY
- Follow the organiser’s participation guidance.
- Ask permission before photographing, recording or publishing other participants.
- Keep the organiser’s contact information available for practical questions.

AFTERWARDS
- Keep your notes and record one action you want to follow up.
- Ask where approved learning materials or updates will be shared.
- For ASIF events, confirm current details directly with the foundation.
